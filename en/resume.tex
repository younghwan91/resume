\section{Summary}
\small{
PhD in numerical optimization (Mechanical Engineering, University of Pretoria) who formulates industrial problems as optimization problems and ships them as production products and in-house solutions. Graduate research (MEng, PhD) covered gradient-only line searches for stochastic or discontinuous objectives, sub-dimensional surrogates for high-dimensional problems under a limited evaluation budget, and combinatorial sensor placement over $\sim$$10^{26}$ candidate layouts.

\vspace{2pt}\noindent At bitsensing, cut fusion RMSE by \textbf{51\%} with \textbf{camera/radar fusion perception} (time synchronization, reprojection-error-minimizing calibration) and built multi-object tracking of up to 256 concurrent tracks, shipped to \textbf{mass production} ($\sim$\textbf{200 units in 8 countries}); also built 3D radar perception (\textbf{PV-RCNN}, \textbf{TensorRT INT8} on NVIDIA Jetson) and deep-learning DoA super-resolution (\textbf{2$\times$} angular resolution). At Doosan Robotics, owns the \textbf{robotic sanding solution}: generates surfaces from raw 3D scans with CAD-free fast surface fitting, then optimizes sanding paths on them with \textbf{NVIDIA PhysicsNeMo} surrogates (\textbf{$\sim$15,000$\times$} faster than FEM), bringing measured Ra within \textbf{7\%} of target --- an industrial application of the PhD surrogate research, with training data generated by an FEM-calibrated NVIDIA Newton simulator.

\vspace{2pt}\noindent Now building \textbf{LLM agent tool orchestration}, in which the agent decides the call order of vision, surface-fitting, and sanding tools, focusing on \textbf{combining optimization with LLM agents}. As a personal project, open-sourced a quant stack for portfolio/strategy optimization and overfitting control, which led to selection as Next Securities \textbf{API Development Ambassador} (2026). \textbf{6 peer-reviewed papers}, \textbf{10 patent applications}.
}
